My Approach

I currently believe that my approach to the STEAM projects I undertake is very comprehensive. I say this because I strive to go beyond just the theoretical side; I first make a concerted effort to master the subject matter, which then enables me to develop a complete project based on what I have learned. I do this because I am a pragmatic learner—I learn much more effectively through hands-on project work than by reading theory alone.

I feel that this approach has helped me make significant progress in a short amount of time. I have learned things on my own that I am certain will be a great help when I pursue a degree in Mechatronics Engineering at university. Furthermore, I have found the learning process itself to be far more enjoyable than I ever imagined it could be. I now have a clear goal regarding the projects I want to undertake: I want to build substantial projects that I am passionate about—not just for the sake of creation, but to overcome challenges and ensure that I never stop learning.
